Art and sounds of the Arcade's phone games (web/src/modules/arcade/engine/lpc.ts cuts the sprites
out, engine/sfx.ts plays the sounds). Downloaded unchanged except for the file names, from
OpenGameArt. They are NOT under Homeboard's MIT license: each keeps its own license and authors
(and, for the LPC sheets, the full credits file that came with it). Everything else in the games
(backgrounds, knives, the log, the wings, the road, the moles, the coins, the bomb...) is drawn in
code for Homeboard.
