Art of the town's shops on the TV (the clothes shop and the barber, web/src/modules/world/shopfronts.ts)
Downloaded unchanged except for the file names, from Eliza Wyatt's LPC Revised collection. Each
entry names the files, the authors, the license and where they come from. Everything here is
OGA-BY 3.0 (https://static.opengameart.org/OGA-BY-3.0.txt).
The houses the shops are built on are the square's (see web/public/crowd/env/CREDITS.txt). The
shopfronts' woodwork, the sign icons (shirt, scissors) and the barber pole are drawn in code for
Homeboard, in the palettes of these sheets.
